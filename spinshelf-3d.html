Concept dimensions

Front opening, floor to top
45 to 120 cm
Cabinet diameter
68 cm
Base column diameter
40 cm
Panel height
about 70 cm
Quarter turn
about 3 s

These numbers are working assumptions for the model, not tested values. The opening sits inside the usual seated forward-reach range (about 38 to 122 cm), and the narrow base leaves open knee space for a wheelchair. Also in the concept but not modelled here: a weight limit per section, removable bins and plug-in power. Check the numbers in the concept test.
